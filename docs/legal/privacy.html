Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED · 2026-08-26

1. What we process

To route a request we necessarily process its content in transit. By default we do not store prompt or completion content after the response is delivered. We retain metadata — token counts, model id, endpoint, latency, and status — for billing and reliability.

2. Optional logging

You can enable content logging per API key for debugging. Logged content is encrypted at rest, visible only to your organization, and deletable at any time.

3. Zero-retention routing

Keys with the zero-retention policy route only to providers that contractually commit to no content retention and no training on API traffic. The catalog labels qualifying endpoints.

4. Upstream providers

Requests are fulfilled by the provider serving your chosen model. Each provider processes content under its own terms; we surface each endpoint's data policy in the catalog and enforce your policy filters at routing time.

5. Account data

We store your email, billing details, and usage records for as long as your account is active. We do not sell personal data. Payment card data is handled by our payment processor and never touches our servers.

6. Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3). Content in optional logs is encrypted at rest (AES-256). Access is audited. Report vulnerabilities to security@modelaxis.ai.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your data. Write to privacy@modelaxis.ai and we will respond within 30 days.
