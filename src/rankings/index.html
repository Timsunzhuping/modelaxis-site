Rankings

Token share across the axis over the last 30 days — a live picture of which models developers actually ship with, not a benchmark.

Method

Share is computed from tokens billed through ModelAxis routing, normalized within the selected category. Private and zero-retention traffic is counted in aggregate only.

Reading the bars

Cobalt is the category leader; every other bar is neutral. Share shifts fast — a price cut or a new release moves the board within days.

Use it live

Route to the current leader automatically with modelaxis/auto, or pin any model on this board by its id.
